Prepared for Synmerco job on intent 5c567ad5-77c5-4ccc-a207-a1acc743b3bd (escrow esc_4797e3fb525449c2b92b3f7a, bid 634). Subjects: Jabil Inc.; Raymond James Financial, Inc.; Darden Restaurants, Inc.; Roper Technologies, Inc.; Publix Super Markets, Inc. Company parties only; no individual was searched or profiled.

Method: every search was run on the CourtListener RECAP docket search API (search type d, federal dockets) with a party-name filter, party:("<variant>"), the window filter filed_after=2021-10-22 (five years before the retrieval date, 2026-10-22), and ordering order_by=dateFiled desc. No API token was used and no key appears in this file. For each subject, three party-name variants were searched (exact name, name without suffix, common short form); the counts below are the count values returned by each query, reported per variant and not merged, because the shorter forms also match other parties that share a word. The case table for a subject lists the ten most recently filed results of that subject's exact-name party search, in the order returned. Role is read from the case caption: the subject on the plaintiff side of "v." is plaintiff, on the defendant side is defendant, and a subject that is a listed party but does not appear on either side of the caption is other. Status is open when the docket record's dateTerminated is null, and otherwise the termination date shown is given. A nature-of-suit or cause field that the docket record shows as an empty string is reported as not shown. Every figure, date, name and identifier in the body appears in a verbatim raw excerpt in the Sources section, printed directly under its query URL.
